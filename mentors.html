My name is Julianna Lazono. I graduated from San Joaquin Delta College in May 2026 and will be transferring to Chico State to pursue a bachelor's degree in Public Administration. Although I began my college journey as a nursing major, my experiences serving both the campus and the community led me to discover my passion for public service, advocacy, and creating opportunities for others.
