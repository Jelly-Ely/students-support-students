Meet Our Mentors

Real students. Real experiences. Real support.

Our mentors come from different schools, majors, backgrounds, and educational pathways, but they share one goal: helping other students feel less alone while navigating higher education.

Student Mentors

Find someone who understands your journey.

Whether you're navigating community college, transferring, applying for financial aid, choosing a major, adjusting to university life, or simply figuring out what comes next, our mentors are here to share what they've learned.

Not sure who to reach out to?

You don't have to choose on your own. Tell us what you need help with and we'll help connect you with a mentor who matches your questions and goals.
