Who We Are

Students helping students move forward.

Students Support Students is a student-led nonprofit built around a simple idea: students who have been through the college process can make it easier for those coming next.

Our Story

We started with a simple idea: students have a lot to teach each other.

College comes with a lot of questions, and finding the right answers is not always easy. Students Support Students creates a space where students can learn from peers who have already navigated many of those same experiences.

Our mentors bring their own experiences with community college, transferring, university life, financial aid, applications, academics, career preparation, and finding opportunities.

Whether someone needs a resource, has a question, or simply wants to hear from another student who understands, our goal is to make guidance easier to find and less intimidating to ask for.

Our Mission

Make educational guidance easier to access.

Students Support Students connects students with peer mentorship, educational resources, and practical guidance that can help them navigate their educational journey.

We want students to have a place where asking questions feels comfortable and where support comes from people who understand the student experience.

Our Vision

No student should have to figure it all out alone.

We envision a community where students share what they have learned, support one another, and make educational information easier for the next student to access.

When students help students, knowledge that once felt difficult to find can become something that is shared.
