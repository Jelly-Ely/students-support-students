Our Story

We know college can feel like a lot.

Applications. Financial aid. Transferring. Classes. Careers. Finding resources. Finding your people.

Sometimes you don't even know what you're supposed to ask.

Students Support Students exists to make those moments a little easier. We connect students with other students who have been there, along with resources and opportunities that can help them take their next step.

Because sometimes the person who can help you most is someone who recently had the exact same question.

Our Mission

Make educational guidance easier to access.

We connect students with peer mentorship, practical resources, educational support, and people who understand the journey so they can make informed decisions about what comes next.

02 ✦

Our Vision

No student should have to figure it all out alone.

We envision a community where students don't need connections, insider knowledge, or all the right answers to find support, understand their options, and move forward.

What We're About

Good support starts with how we treat people.

These values shape how we show up for students and for each other.

01

Student-Led

Students have lived experience worth sharing. What we've learned can make someone else's path easier.

02

Accessible

Finding useful information shouldn't feel like another assignment. Support should be clear and easy to reach.

03

Encouraging

There are no embarrassing questions here. Asking for help is part of figuring things out.

04

Community

We share resources, opportunities, and experiences because students are stronger when we help each other.

05

Inclusive

There isn't one right path through education. Every student's background and journey deserves respect.

06

Trustworthy

We believe in honest peer guidance and connecting students with information and resources they can rely on.
