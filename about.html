About Us

Students supporting students through every step.

Students Support Students is a student-led nonprofit created to make higher education feel more accessible, understandable, and welcoming.

Who We Are

Support created by students who understand the journey.

We help high school seniors, college students, and transfer students navigate applications, financial aid, campus life, resources, and important educational transitions.

Because our program is designed and operated by students, participants receive guidance from people who have recently faced many of the same questions and challenges.

Our Mission

Empower students through guidance and connection.

We provide peer mentorship, trusted resources, educational workshops, and personalized support so students can make informed decisions about their future.

Our Vision

A community where no student figures it out alone.

We imagine a future where every student has access to encouragement, reliable information, and someone they can turn to when education feels overwhelming.

Our Values

What guides the way we support students.

These values shape every conversation, resource, workshop, and mentorship connection.

01

Student Leadership

Students bring valuable knowledge and lived experience that can help others move forward.

02

Accessibility

Support and information should be understandable, easy to find, and available to every student.

03

Encouragement

Students deserve support that builds confidence and helps them recognize their strengths.

04

Community

Strong communities grow when students share resources, experiences, and opportunities.

05

Inclusion

Every student should feel welcomed, respected, and valued regardless of their background.

06

Trust

We provide honest guidance, respect privacy, and connect students with reliable resources.
