Our Story

We know how overwhelming college can feel when you don't know where to start.

Students Support Students was created around a simple idea: students shouldn't have to figure out higher education alone.

From applications and financial aid to transferring, finding campus resources, and adjusting to college life, many of the questions students face have already been navigated by someone just a few steps ahead.

We bring those students together—creating a community where experience can become guidance, questions are welcomed, and finding your next step feels a little easier.

01

Our Mission

Make educational guidance easier to access.

We connect students with peer mentorship, practical resources, educational support, and people who understand the journey so they can make informed decisions about what comes next.

02

Our Vision

A future where students don't have to figure it out alone.

We envision a community where students don't need connections, insider knowledge, or all the right answers to find support, understand their options, and move forward.

What We Believe

The values behind our work.

How we support students matters just as much as the information we share. These principles guide our mentorship, resources, outreach, and community.

01

Student Leadership

Students bring valuable knowledge and lived experience that can help someone else move forward.

02

Accessibility

Support and information should be understandable, approachable, and easy for students to find.

03

Encouragement

Students deserve guidance that builds confidence instead of making them feel behind for asking questions.

04

Community

We become stronger when students share experiences, resources, opportunities, and what they've learned.

05

Inclusion

Every student deserves to feel welcomed, respected, and valued regardless of their background or educational path.

06

Trust

We believe in honest peer guidance, respecting students' experiences, and connecting people with reliable resources.
