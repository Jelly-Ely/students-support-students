Our Story

We started with a simple idea: students have a lot to teach each other.

Applications. Financial aid. Transferring. Classes. Careers. Campus life.

There are so many parts of college that can be confusing, especially when you're experiencing them for the first time.

Students Support Students brings together students who are willing to share what they've learned with students who could use that support.

Whether it's answering a question, sharing a resource, or helping someone understand their options, our goal is to make navigating education feel a little less overwhelming.

Students supporting students.
01 Our Mission

Make educational guidance easier to access.

We connect students with peer mentorship, practical resources, educational support, and people who understand the journey so students can make informed decisions about what comes next.

02 Our Vision

No student should have to figure it all out alone.

We envision a community where students don't need connections, insider knowledge, or all the right answers to find support, understand their options, and move forward.
