A Quick Note

We're students supporting students.

Our mentors share peer guidance based on their own educational experiences. We aren't a replacement for academic advisors, financial aid professionals, legal professionals, mental health professionals, or other specialized services.

If your question requires expertise outside our role, we'll do our best to point you toward an appropriate office, organization, or trusted resource.
