Common Questions

What students usually want to know.

Is support free?

Yes. Students Support Students provides mentorship, resources, and guidance without charging students.

Who can request help?

High school, community college, transfer, and university students are welcome to request support.

Do I need to attend a specific school?

No. You can explore our resources or request guidance regardless of the school you attend.
