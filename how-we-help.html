Common Questions

Questions about getting support?

Is support free?

Yes. Students Support Students provides mentorship, resources, and guidance without charging students.

Who can reach out for support?

High school, community college, transfer, and university students are welcome to contact our team for support.

Do I need to attend a specific school?

No. You can explore our resources or reach out for guidance regardless of the school you attend.

Do I need to choose a mentor before contacting you?

No. Tell us what you're looking for and we can help identify a mentor or resource that fits your needs.

Can I contact a mentor directly?

Yes. You can browse our mentor directory and contact mentors using the email provided on their profile.

What if you can't answer my question?

Our mentors are peers, not professional advisors. When something is outside our experience, we'll do our best to point you toward an appropriate campus office, organization, or trusted resource.
