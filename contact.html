Questions

Before you reach out.

Here are a few quick answers about contacting Students Support Students.

Do I need to attend a specific school to ask for support?

No. Students Support Students is designed to support students across different educational paths and institutions.

Can I contact a mentor directly?

You can browse our mentor directory to learn more about our mentors. If you're unsure who to connect with, contact our team and we can help point you in the right direction.

Can organizations contact Students Support Students?

Yes. We welcome messages from schools, student organizations, nonprofits, and community organizations interested in partnerships, events, workshops, or other collaborations.

Can I submit a scholarship or student opportunity?

Yes. Use the contact form and choose “Resource or Opportunity” so our team can review it.

Not Sure Where to Start?

That's okay. Just ask.

You don't need to know exactly what kind of support you need. Send us your question and we'll help you figure out your next step.
