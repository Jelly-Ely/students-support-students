Before You Reach Out

You may find your answer here.

These are a few common questions about support, mentorship, and partnerships.

How do I request help from a mentor?

Complete the Request Support form and explain what you need help with. Our team will review your request and connect you with an appropriate mentor or resource.

Can I recommend a resource for the website?

Yes. Use the contact form and select Resource Recommendation as the subject.

How can my school or organization partner with you?

Select Community Partnership in the contact form and tell us about your organization and the type of collaboration you have in mind.

Where can I apply to become a mentor?

Visit the Get Involved page to learn about mentorship, volunteering, and other ways to support students.
