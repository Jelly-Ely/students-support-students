Before You Reach Out

You may find your answer here.

How do I ask for student support?

Use the contact form above and select Student Support. Tell us what you're navigating and our team can help identify a relevant mentor, resource, or next step.

Can I recommend a resource or opportunity?

Yes. Select Resource or Opportunity in the contact form and tell us about the resource, scholarship, program, event, or opportunity you'd like us to consider sharing.

How can my school or organization partner with you?

Select Community Partnership and tell us about your organization and the type of collaboration, event, workshop, or partnership you have in mind.

How can I become a mentor or volunteer?

Visit our Get Involved page to learn about student mentorship, volunteering, workshops, partnerships, and other ways to contribute.

Can I contact you directly by email?

Yes. You can email our team directly at studentsupportquestions@gmail.com.
