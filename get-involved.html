Why Get Involved?

Your experience can become someone else's resource.

Sometimes a conversation, shared resource, or piece of encouragement can make an unfamiliar process feel much more manageable.

01

Share What You've Learned

Turn your own experiences with college, applications, transferring, financial aid, campus life, and opportunities into support for someone else.

02

Build Your Skills

Develop communication, mentorship, leadership, collaboration, outreach, and other skills while contributing to your community.

03

Strengthen Community

Help create a culture where students share information, open doors for one another, and don't have to navigate education alone.

Student Mentorship

What does being a mentor look like?

Our mentors use their own experiences to help students feel more informed and supported while navigating higher education.

You don't need to have every answer. Mentorship is about listening, sharing what you've learned, helping students identify useful resources, and being honest when a question is outside your experience.

01

Share Your Experience

Talk openly about what you've learned from your own educational journey.

02

Support, Don't Pressure

Help students explore their options while respecting that their goals and decisions belong to them.

03

Know Your Limits

When something requires professional or specialized guidance, help students find an appropriate resource rather than guessing.
